Questions to ask and details to gather

Roofing work depends on what’s happening at the home. A first discussion can help identify what information is missing and what kind of work may be appropriate. Ask the contractor to explain the reasoning behind a recommendation, whether the work is limited to one area, and what the next step will be. The specific scope should be based on the roof, not on guesswork.

Use this short checklist before you call:

  • Note the full West Milford address.
  • Describe the roof concern in your own words.
  • Record when you first noticed it and any changes since.
  • Take photos from a safe spot on the ground, if useful.
  • Write down questions about scope, timing, and what happens next.

A common mistake is treating a contractor’s first impression as a complete work plan. Ask for details you can review. Another is climbing onto the roof to get a closer look. Leave roof access to a professional.
